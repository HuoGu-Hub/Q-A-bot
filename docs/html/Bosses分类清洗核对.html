第 1 块 · wiki-fell-dragon-youngling · 1093 字

The Fell Dragon Youngling is a boss in Enshrouded.
It is a unique boss that is only found at Howling Peak. Defeating it is an objective of the quest Slaying The Corrupted Beast.
It drops the key ingredient for the Flame Altar's eighth upgrade, the Fell Dragon Youngling Head.
Locations
Howling Peak, Albaneve Summits
Abilities
Fell Fire Meteors — Spins in a circle unleashing a wave of Fell Fire Meteors
Fell Fire Breath — Breathes in and unleashes Fell Fire Breath. When in the middle of the platform the dragon will rotate spewing fire at the platform. Outside the platform the dragon will breathe fire in front of it from left to right.
Fell Fire Strafe — Flies forward breathing fire in front of itself, flying across the platform.
Wing Beat — The dragon will flap its wings knocking you back.
Summon Fell Draconian Vultures
Strategy
The dragon takes reduced damage when it isn't stunned, so prioritize filling its stun bar and dodging its attacks rather than dealing damage. You can fill up its stun bar using Stun Arrows, lightning spells like Chain Lightning or Eternal Chain Lightning.

第 2 块 · wiki-fell-dragon-youngling-2 · 1025 字

The Fell Fire Meteors it summons can be deflected back to fill its stun bar as well. The dragon will lean its head back before breathing fire, during this time you can fly to the other side of the platform and avoid this mechanic. Watch the dragon closely as it spins around to summon its Fell Fire Meteors and get in place to deflect them back. After its stun bar the dragon will fall to the ground, if you're underneath or near it you will be knocked back. During this time deal as much damage as you can. After it wakes up it will summon Fell Draconian Vultures and resume its attack patterns. Defeat those and fill the stun bar again, repeat until it is defeated.
If you get hit by an attack and get lit on fire you can dodge or blink to put out the fire.

It is a unique boss that is only found at Howling Peak. Defeating it is an objective of the quest {{quest+icon|Slaying The Corrupted Beast}}.

==Locations==
#*Howling Peak, [[Albaneve Summits]]

==Abilities==
'''Fell Fire Meteors''' — Spins in a circle unleashing a wave of Fell Fire Meteors

'''Fell Fire Breath''' — Breathes in and unleashes Fell Fire Breath. When in the middle of the platform the dragon will rotate spewing fire at the platform. Outside the platform the dragon will breathe fire in front of it from left to right.

'''Fell Fire Strafe''' — Flies forward breathing fire in front of itself, flying across the platform.

'''Wing Beat''' — The dragon will flap its wings knocking you back.

'''Summon [[Fell Draconian Vulture]]s'''

==Strategy==
The dragon takes reduced damage when it isn't stunned, so prioritize filling its stun bar and dodging its attacks rather than dealing damage. You can fill up its stun bar using [[Stun Arrow|Stun Arrows]], lightning spells like [[Chain Lightning]] or [[Eternal Chain Lightning]]. 

The Fell Fire Meteors it summons can be deflected back to fill its stun bar as well. The dragon will lean its head back before breathing fire, during this time you can fly to the other side of the platform and avoid this mechanic. Watch the dragon closely as it spins around to summon its Fell Fire Meteors and get in place to deflect them back. After its stun bar the dragon will fall to the ground, if you're underneath or near it you will be knocked back. During this time deal as much damage as you can. After it wakes up it will summon [[Fell Draconian Vulture|Fell Draconian Vultures]] and resume its attack patterns. Defeat those and fill the stun bar again, repeat until it is defeated.

If you get hit by an attack and get lit on fire you can dodge or blink to put out the fire.
